# Modification Approval

## Preconditions
Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/engineering/DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md`, and the applicable protocol(s) in `docs/engineering/references/`. Confirm this is the active stage and prior gates are satisfied. If prerequisites are missing, stop and ask.

## Procedure
1. Confirm Stage 1–3 approvals and evidence; identify exact proposed behavior changes and affected boundaries.
2. Present alternatives with tradeoffs, compatibility/security/data/performance impacts, risks and exclusions; recommend only with stated evidence.
3. Distinguish required modifications from optional improvements; avoid hidden refactors.
4. Record a revisioned modification proposal with affected areas, assumptions and acceptance impacts.
5. Ask for explicit approval of the proposal revision; partial or conditional approval must be reflected in a revised proposal.
6. Stop until approved. Approval here does not authorize implementation.

## Output and exit
Provide the stage deliverable, evidence labels and paths, uncertainties, status, and exact next approval or action. Apply the workflow's stage transition; never infer approval.
